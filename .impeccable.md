## Design Context

### Users
Working professionals in India planning major life milestones (marriage, baby, house, retirement). They use the app on a laptop during daytime planning sessions — reviewing monthly surplus, adjusting SIPs, and checking whether goals are on track.

### Brand Personality
Calm, confident, editorial. Trustworthy like a well-designed financial brief — not a trading terminal or spreadsheet dump. Numbers always appear with narrative context.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light mode primary with warm sage-teal tinted neutrals
- Typography: Libre Franklin (UI/body) + Source Serif 4 (headings/display)
- Anti-references: dark neon dashboards, gradient text, side-stripe cards, hero-metric card grids, cold gray SaaS palettes
- INR-first formatting with Indian numbering conventions

### Design Principles
1. Goal timelines over raw lists — show where you're headed, not just what you spent
2. Progressive disclosure — start simple, reveal complexity on demand
3. One confident number per insight — avoid four identical stat cards
4. Solid colors for emphasis — no gradient text or decorative chrome
5. Editorial spacing rhythm — tight groupings, generous section breaks
