## Design Context

### Users
Working professionals in India planning major life milestones (marriage, baby, house, retirement). They use the app on a laptop during planning sessions and on a phone (installed PWA) to log ledger transactions and check obligations.

### Brand Personality
Affirmative, precise, a little theatrical — CRED's NeoPop energy applied to personal planning. Feels like a premium fintech product: black surfaces, sharp geometry, tactile 3D presses, confident numbers. Never playful-cute, never spreadsheet-grey.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Design system: NeoPop (see DESIGN.md). Dark mode default; light mode supported.
- Shape: zero border radius; elevation only via the 3px 45° "plunk" edge.
- Colour: monochrome black/white/greys; acid lime `#E5FE40` brand accent; green/red/orange/blue only as semantic signals.
- Typography: Manrope (UI, 400–800) + Instrument Serif (display/page titles) + Geist Mono (card numbers). Uppercase tracked caps labels for metadata.
- Anti-references: rounded SaaS cards, glassmorphism, gradients, glow rings, soft shadows, pastel neutrals, emoji icons.
- INR-first formatting with Indian numbering conventions.

### Design Principles
1. Sharp, not soft — square corners, 1px strokes, plunk edges.
2. One confident number per insight — tabular, extra-bold, with a caps label.
3. Colour is a signal, not decoration.
4. Presses are mechanical — 3px translate into the plunk in 120 ms.
5. Progressive disclosure — start with the summary, reveal detail on demand.
